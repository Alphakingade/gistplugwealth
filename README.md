# GistPlugWealth

You are building GistPlugWealth, a Nigerian digital finance, personal finance and money-making publication.



IMPORTANT:

Do not treat this as a generic blog template.

Build it as a real, production-ready MVP for a Nigerian finance and opportunity media brand, with a clear path for future expansion.



The website must feel intentionally designed, trustworthy, modern, Nigerian and content-focused.



==================================================

1. PRODUCT IDENTITY

==================================================



Brand name:

GistPlugWealth



Tagline:

"Inform. Inspire. Increase."



Brand positioning:

GistPlugWealth is a Nigerian digital finance and opportunity platform that helps people make smarter financial decisions, discover legitimate ways to earn money, save money and build wealth.



Core brand message:



INFORM

Help people understand money, finance, opportunities, apps, businesses and financial developments.



INSPIRE

Encourage people to improve their financial situation and take practical action.



INCREASE

Help readers increase their financial knowledge, income opportunities, savings and overall financial growth.



The brand should feel:

- Nigerian

- Practical

- Helpful

- Aspirational

- Trustworthy

- Friendly

- Modern

- Educational



Avoid making the brand feel:

- Like a traditional bank

- Overly corporate

- Like a cryptocurrency scam

- Like a "get rich quick" website

- Overly technical

- Cluttered
